Immune-metabolic trajectories delineate subgroups in paediatric long COVID

2026-05-04
Category: Mechanism AI relevance: 85/100

The study identifies immunometabolic subgroups in pediatric long COVID with structured trajectories over time, influenced by disease phase and EBV imprinting. Recovery-associated features are linked to milder impairment.

Here the authors show in a longitudinal cohort with up to 3 years follow-up that paediatric long COVID comprises temporally structured immunometabolic subgroups shaped by disease phase, EBV imprinting and autoreactivity. Recovery-associated features track with milder impairment and support stratification.
